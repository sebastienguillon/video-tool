# Logo directions

Candidates for the app icon, in the author's lime (`#a8c70b`) on the olive black that SG Tag uses
(`#131510`). Each one comes on both grounds: `-dark` (lime on olive black) and `-lime` (olive black
on lime). None is the app icon yet: once one is chosen, `scripts/build-icons.ts` draws it, with
hand-drawn 16 and 32 px versions, and these files go.

Rounds 2 and 3 are here; round 1 (brackets, snip, trimmed ring, batch, film strip, signature) is in
this folder's history.

| File               | Direction          | Idea                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `g-sg-play`        | SG play            | The SG logo, the ring of its G filled as a play button (`-open`: left open)  |
| `h-sg-film`        | SG film            | The SG logo's circles perforated like film wound on reels                    |
| `i-sg-viewfinder`  | SG viewfinder      | The SG logo framed by a camera viewfinder's corner marks                     |
| `j-sg-clapper`     | SG clapper         | A clapperboard, the SG logo on its slate                                     |
| `k-iris`           | Iris               | A three-blade camera iris whose opening is the play triangle                 |
| `l-waveform`       | Waveform           | A play triangle made of soundtrack bars, the first and last ones trimmed     |
| `m-sg-film-edges`  | SG on film         | The SG logo between two film edges punched with holes                        |
| `m-sg-film-holes`  | SG on film         | The SG logo with a column of perforations on each side, no edges drawn       |
| `m-sg-film-frame`  | SG on film         | One film frame, between perforations, holding the SG logo                    |
| `n-sg-play-behind` | SG with a big play | A big, centred play triangle over the SG logo drawn faintly                  |
| `n-sg-play-over`   | SG with a big play | A big, centred play triangle over the full SG logo, outlined to stand off it |
| `n-sg-play-inside` | SG with a big play | A big play triangle holding the SG logo                                      |

G to J, M and N draw the SG logo exactly as `sebastienguillon - logo alt.svg` does, on its grid of
100-radius circles.
