# Logo directions

Six candidates for the app icon, in the author's lime (`#a8c70b`) on the olive black that SG Tag
uses (`#131510`). Each one comes on both grounds: `-dark` (lime on olive black) and `-lime` (olive
black on lime). None is the app icon yet: once one is chosen, `scripts/build-icons.ts` draws it, with
hand-drawn 16 and 32 px versions, and these files go.

| File          | Direction       | Idea                                                                    |
| ------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `a-brackets`  | Brackets        | The current icon, recoloured: a play triangle between two trim brackets |
| `b-snip`      | Snip            | A play triangle with its end snipped off                                |
| `c-ring`      | Trimmed ring    | A play button whose ring is cut away at the top and the bottom          |
| `d-batch`     | Batch           | Three video frames stacked, the front one playing                       |
| `e-strip`     | Keep the middle | A film strip with its start and end cut away                            |
| `f-signature` | Signature       | The SG logo's ring, ear and tint around a play triangle                 |

From the SG logo: the 73° slant of its lower cut (the cuts in B and E), its 40-on-100 stroke, 45° ear
and 25% tint (F), and its circular arcs (C).
